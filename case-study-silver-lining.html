Case Study · Silver Lining Mentoring

10x More Mentors. Same Mission.

Challenge
Finding the right people for the hardest kind of commitment

Silver Lining Mentoring pairs volunteer mentors with youth in foster care across Greater Boston. Their mission is urgent and the need is real — but recruiting qualified, committed mentors requires reaching an audience that's both willing and ready to show up consistently for a kid.

Generic awareness campaigns weren't working. They needed to reach the right people with the right message at the moment they were open to it.

Solution
Human stories, direct calls to action, trusted channels

We led with the people, not the program. Content centered on the real impact of mentorship — specific, honest, and personal — placed inside editorial and email contexts where our audience was already in a giving, community-minded headspace.

By pairing storytelling with frictionless calls to action and distributing across Silver Lining's most relevant audience segments, we made it easy for the right people to take the first step.

Impact
1 million impressions. 10x qualified leads. 19% conversion.

The campaign drove 1 million total impressions and a tenfold increase in qualified mentor leads. Of those who expressed interest, 19% completed a full application — a conversion rate that reflected not just reach, but fit.

Silver Lining didn't just get more applicants. They got better ones.
