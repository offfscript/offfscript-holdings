Inspiration & Challenge

Boston Landing needed to increase awareness and drive foot traffic to its events and neighborhood experiences — competing for attention in a city full of options, without a widely recognized brand identity.

Execution & Creativity

We positioned Boston Landing as a go-to destination for dining, drinks, shopping, and entertainment, using its events to attract audiences and build long-term neighborhood affinity.

Leveraging The Boston Calendar's event-focused audience and multi-channel reach, we created a sustained presence that consistently surfaced Boston Landing across the moments when people were deciding what to do — turning event awareness into neighborhood loyalty.
